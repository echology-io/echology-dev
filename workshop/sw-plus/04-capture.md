## How to write in here

Verbatim over paraphrase, always. Their exact words are the asset; a cleaned-up
version of their words is a generic product. If you only half-caught a sentence, mark
it `~` and write what you got — a partial quote flagged as partial is usable, a
smoothed-over guess is not.

One line per capture, tag first, attribution and clock at the end:

```
[PAIN] "we redraw the same intersection detail four times a year" — York, T+31
```

| Tag | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| `[PAIN]` | A named friction. What actually happens, not what should. |
| `[VOCAB]` | Their word for something. Internal names, project phases, roles, systems. |
| `[ALIGN]` | They described a capability that already exists, unprompted. Verbatim, always. |
| `[PILOT]` | Something that could be a first project. |
| `[SKEPTIC]` | An objection, a hesitation, a pointed question. Record it as stated, not as answered. |
| `[POLITICS]` | Who defers to whom. Who interrupts. Who is not in the room but gets cited. |
| `[SYSTEM]` | A named platform, script, file location, integration point. |
| `[REPEAT]` | Third time or more you have heard it. Tag the third occurrence, not the first. |
| `[QUOTE]` | Worth reading back to them in the follow-up. |
| `[?]` | You do not understand it yet. Ask before you leave. |

Repetition is the primary instrument. Three people describing the same friction in
three different vocabularies is a stronger finding than one person describing it
emphatically.
